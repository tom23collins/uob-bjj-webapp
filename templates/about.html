About the club

The UoB Brazilian Jiu Jitsu club is one of the university's newest clubs. Starting in the second term of 2023 we quickly became an established club at UoB, we now have a full timetable of 5 sessions per week, an elite coaching staff and a diverse team which is always growing.

We accept everybody, no matter who you are or what your goals are; we aim to make our club a place where anybody can train and learn high quality BJJ. Whether you want to compete, or are just looking to give BJJ a go, we can assure you, we have a place for you.

What sets us apart from other clubs is our elite coaching staff and our affiliate team, Royal Jiu Jitsu.

Our sessions are coached by Coach Remy and Coach Ryan, who bring high quality technical coaching and a welcoming training environment for all levels.

With us you'll have the opportunity to compete in local and national level competitions as well as the annual university league competition. And we'll be there with you every step of the way to offer our guidance and support.
